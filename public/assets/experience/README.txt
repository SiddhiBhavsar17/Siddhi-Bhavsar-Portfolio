EXPERIENCE & INTERNSHIP DOCUMENTS DIRECTORY (/public/assets/experience/)

Place your experience documents here:
- Offer letters (e.g. LabMentix_Offer_Letter.pdf)
- Internship certificates (e.g. LabMentix_Certificate.pdf, Tata_Forage_Certificate.pdf)

- Recommendation letters

Each document is explicitly configured with its purpose in:
  src/data/experience.ts

Expected files:
1. LabMentix Offer Letter:
   /public/assets/experience/LabMentix_Offer_Letter.pdf
2. LabMentix Certificate:
   /public/assets/experience/LabMentix_Certificate.pdf
3. Tata Group × Forage Certificate:
   /public/assets/experience/Tata_Forage_Certificate.pdf

Format: PDF
